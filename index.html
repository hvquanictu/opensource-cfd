FAQ

Before you ask.

Is OpenFOAM as accurate as Ansys Fluent or STAR-CCM+?

Accuracy comes from the physics models, mesh quality and validation — not from the software brand. Every study includes a mesh-independence check and a comparison with experiments or reference solutions; for example, our oil-jet solver is within 3.0 % of 20 experiments.

Is my geometry and data kept confidential?

Yes. We sign an NDA before receiving any file. Client projects are never published without written permission.

What do I need to provide?

A short description and the question you want answered is enough to start. Later: CAD (STEP/STL), operating conditions, material properties and any measured data you have.

How much does it cost?

Every study gets a fixed price after the free review. A typical study costs less than one prototype iteration — and far less than one year of a commercial CFD licence.

How long does it take?

Feasibility review: 24 h. Standard study: 2–6 weeks depending on complexity. Express timelines are possible.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We work in English, French and Vietnamese, remotely, with clients in any time zone.
